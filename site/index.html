Purpose

An assistant who’s already read your email

Every assistant app makes you paste the email in. Copy the thread, switch windows, explain the context, paste the answer back. Dispatch lives where the email is, so the assistant has already read it.

And it isn’t a bolt-on. Dispatch is the email client with all of Codex inside: the full Codex you already pay for, with your inbox in front of it. Every plugin, tool, and model your Codex has works from the email you’re reading. Ask for the gist, draft the reply, pull the numbers from the attachment, check your calendar, open the repo. Anything your Codex can reach, it can do from here.

Each conversation gets its own Codex thread, so context follows the email instead of the chat. Codex can read, search, and draft through Gmail, but every send, delete, and recipient change waits for you. You still press Send.

Because it’s open source, you can read exactly what it does with your mail, run it from source, or change any part of it.

  • A Codex thread per conversation
  • Every Codex plugin you’ve connected
  • Plans and tool calls you can watch
  • Steer mid-task or stop it
  • Your models, your account, your config
  • Unified queue across Gmail accounts
  • Drafts you review before sending
  • Send, delete and forward need your approval
  • Attachments open in the native app
  • Gmail-style keyboard shortcuts
  • Multi-select and drag to folders
  • Undo for archive, trash and moves
  • Local SQLite index for fast folders
  • Catches up after sleep and reconnects
  • New-mail chime, only for new mail
  • Sandboxed web view for links
  • Native macOS app
